# Website Requirements & Guiding Principles

## PDF-to-Audiobook Service Landing Page

---

## Project Overview

A Next.js website for a PDF-to-audiobook conversion service that transforms any PDF into an audiobook using AI voices. The service is positioned as affordable ($5/month) and mobile-first with a focus on accessibility and convenience.

---

## Design Principles & UI/UX Guidelines

_Based on reference websites: mu.se, ada.cx, zenwood.studio, allwhere.co_

### Visual Design Approach Options

#### Option 1: Bold & Energetic (mu.se style)

- **Color palette:** Dark themes with vibrant accent colors (red, orange gradients)
- **Typography:** Condensed, heavy sans-serif fonts for headlines
- **Visual elements:** Hand-drawn scribbles/overlays for energy
- **Layout:** Magazine-spread feeling with huge typography
- **Product presentation:** Color-coded cards with bold backgrounds
- **Considerations:** Ensure contrast accessibility for low vision users

#### Option 2: Modern & Professional (ada.cx style)

- **Color palette:** Multi-color approach (yellow, green, orange, blue)
- **Typography:** Modern geometric sans-serif (like "Roobert")
- **Approach:** Clean, contemporary AI-SaaS aesthetic
- **Tone:** Lively and approachable while maintaining professionalism

#### Option 3: Playful & Human (zenwood.studio style)

- **Color palette:** Pastel backgrounds with bright accent buttons
- **Visual elements:** Scrapbook-like assets (compass drawings, paperclips, Polaroid frames)
- **Layout:** Generous spacing, sticky navigation
- **Cards:** Rounded cards resembling sticky notes
- **Tone:** Handcrafted, charming, and personal

#### Option 4: Corporate-Friendly (allwhere.co style)

- **Color palette:** Muted palette (beige, pale yellow, teal) with mustard accents
- **Illustrations:** Hand-drawn style making complex processes approachable
- **Layout:** Two-column layouts, generous white space
- **Tone:** Corporate polish with playful illustrations

### UI Patterns & Elements

#### Navigation

- **Sticky top navigation** with logo and key links
- **Primary CTA buttons** prominently placed (contrasting colors)
- **Secondary actions** (login/signup) with outlined button style
- **Quick nav anchors** in footer

#### Hero Section Design

- **Oversized headlines** with mission/value proposition
- **Visual metaphors** (pocket illustrations, global buildings, etc.)
- **Clear hierarchy** with headline → sub-headline → CTA flow
- **Background options:** Dark with overlay text, light with illustrations, or pastel themes

#### Content Sections

- **Color-coded product/feature cards** for easy differentiation
- **Two-column layouts** (text + illustration/screenshot)
- **Vertical card stacks** with bold background colors
- **Visual step-by-step processes** with icons and connecting lines
- **Testimonial cards** with quotes, names, and company logos

#### Visual Elements

- **Hand-drawn illustrations** to make technical concepts approachable
- **Product screenshots** on mobile/desktop mockups
- **Icon systems** (whimsical or clean geometric)
- **Dotted lines and connectors** for process flows
- **Gradient accents** for section headings and CTAs

#### Interactive Elements

- **Video banners** ("See [product] in action")
- **Chat widgets** (positioned to avoid content overlap on mobile)
- **Hover states** and micro-interactions
- **Multiple CTA placement** throughout page flow

### Typography Guidelines

- **Headlines:** Bold, condensed sans-serif OR modern geometric fonts
- **Body text:** Clean, readable sans-serif
- **Hierarchy:** Clear size/weight differentiation
- **Contrast:** Ensure accessibility compliance

### Color Strategy

- **Primary palette:** Choose 2-3 main brand colors
- **Accent colors:** 1-2 bright colors for CTAs and highlights
- **Background options:** White, light pastels, or dark themes
- **Accessibility:** Maintain sufficient contrast ratios

### Accessibility Considerations

- **Color contrast:** Meet WCAG guidelines (especially for dark themes)
- **Alternative themes:** Consider light/dark mode options
- **Typography:** Ensure readable font sizes and spacing
- **Interactive elements:** Proper focus states and touch targets
- **Mobile optimization:** Avoid overlapping elements

### Mobile-First Considerations

- **Responsive navigation:** Collapsible or simplified on mobile
- **Touch-friendly CTAs:** Adequate button sizes
- **Scroll experience:** Smooth section transitions
- **Content priority:** Most important information first

### Brand Personality Options

1. **Tech-forward & Bold:** Dark themes, vibrant accents, modern typography
2. **Approachable & Friendly:** Pastels, playful illustrations, rounded elements
3. **Professional & Trustworthy:** Muted colors, clean layouts, corporate polish
4. **Creative & Human:** Hand-drawn elements, personal touches, scrapbook aesthetics
